About Us

Three friends who share a deep passion for design, architecture and building.

Scott Keller

Principal

Scott is a creative and intuitive thinker with 25 years of multi-disciplinary experience in various industries such as real estate, entertainment, music and international trade.

He has been serving clients in financing, residential and income-generating properties for over 18 years. He's successfully purchased, fixed and flipped numerous properties across Los Angeles, Portland, OR and Tucson, AZ during this time. He is an accomplished soup-to-nuts developer who has a natural feel for spatial design and a passion to create spaces that feel open and timeless.

This design and development experience; along with construction and financing industry background, enables him to provide a unique value proposition to our clients. His goal is to surpass client's expectations by providing a personalized yet uniquely creative and sensible point of view.

Daryl Vidal

Contractor

An accomplished contractor, Daryl has more than 25 years of experience in the industry. He has an extensive and diverse portfolio that encompasses homes remodelling and construction of multimillion-dollar homes from the ground up. His expertise is recognized not just in the US, but also at a global level.

As a licensed contractor, he has worked in different countries including Saudi Arabia, Australia, Indonesia and New Zealand. During his successful career, Daryl has efficiently managed a variety of construction projects. Through these projects, he closely observed and enjoyed cultural diversity and learned to overcome different challenges. He achieves a sense of fulfilment when he helps the client achieve a unique home design with precision.

Diego Pirona

Design Director

With over 25 years of Design experience across residential, retail and commercial projects, Diego's wholistic approach is an invaluable asset to any Development. For DLA Architects he's coordinated many Residential, Mixed-Use and Custom Fabrication projects throughout California. At Westfield Corporation, he was on Productions Teams for Century City Mall – CA Expansion 220,000sqft and Topanga Mall – CA 970,000sqft. With Frank R. Webb Architects, he worked on Kaiser Permanente's Independence Park conversion of 160,000sqft into their headquarters. He was a National Project Manager at Linane/Drews Architects, locally worked on Banana Republic, Ann Taylor, Robinson's-May, and Macy's.

With his diverse experience in scope and scale, Diego's strength is creating value by truly listening to Clients and assisting guide them at achieving their desired results in often uniquely challenging projects.
